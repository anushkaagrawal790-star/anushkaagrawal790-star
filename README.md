## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 1. 🚀 [AI Virality Predictor & Multi-Platform Optimizer](https://github.com/anushkaagrawal790-star/ai-virality-predictor)
> **An AI-powered "Doctor for Short-Form Videos"**
>
> 🌐 **Live Demo**: [ai-virality-predictor.vercel.app](https://ai-virality-predictor.vercel.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 📖 **API Docs**: [Swagger Docs](https://ai-virality-predictor.onrender.com/docs)

- 📌 **For Non-Tech**: Over 70% of viewers swipe away from short-form videos (Shorts, TikTok, Reels) in the first 3 seconds. This tool acts as a pre-publish diagnostic lab: creators upload their video, and the AI scans motion, sound, and pacing to give a **0–100 Virality Score** and an actionable editing checklist *before* they post.
- ⚙️ **Under the Hood (Tech)**: 
  - **Machine Learning**: HistGradientBoostingRegressor trained on 10,000+ empirical records (^2 = 0.8714$,  = 7.74$).
  - **Computer Vision & Audio**: OpenCV optical flow displacement for hook velocity, Librosa for acoustic energy/tempo.
  - **Full-Stack Stack**: Next.js 14 App Router, Tailwind CSS, FastAPI, Docker, and GitHub Actions CI/CD.
